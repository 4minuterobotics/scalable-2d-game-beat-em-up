# Vision

A platform that lets non-developers build and publish browser-playable 2D games about things personal to them — hometown, family, school, causes they care about — without writing code. Every game ships as a path on the owner's platform site (`platform.com/play/{slug}`), can be monetized via ads and Stripe pay-to-play, and earns the creator a revenue share determined by their subscription tier.

The work is sequenced in two phases:

- **V1 is a tool for the founder** (William) to build a beat-em-up about his hometown.
- **V2 is the platform product** — the editor and schema built in V1 become the commercial product anyone can use.

V1 proves the editor is actually usable by its author before strangers are invited in.
