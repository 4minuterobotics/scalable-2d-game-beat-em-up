# Suggested Subscription Tiers (V2)

Founder is merchant of record; no Stripe Connect. All payments flow through the founder's Stripe, creators receive a revenue share determined by tier.

| Tier | $/mo | Creator revenue share | Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | 0% (platform keeps all) | 1 published game, platform branding, ads on |
| **Creator** | $19 | 50% | 3 games, ads configurable, Stripe pay-to-play allowed |
| **Pro** | $49 | 70% | Unlimited games, custom URL / subdomain (V3), priority moderation, AI sprite credits included (V3) |

Tiers are a starting point — adjust once V2 launches and usage patterns emerge.
